PumpLocal v2

Check your station anytime, from anywhere.

For gas station owners who can't be at the station every day. Ask your pump attendant to snap the meter, and in seconds you'll know whether fuel, sales and cash match. It works on a normal phone, with no computer at the station, even during a brownout or with weak signal.

Status: plan only. Nothing here is built until Johnny approves it.

The owner's problem

📍

You're far away

Many owners live in another city. Johnny runs JCB Gas Station in Sto. Niño, South Cotabato from Tagaytay, about 1,000 km away.

🕔

Checks happen too late

Most stations only count at the end of a shift. Once a gap shows up, the fuel or cash is already gone and it's hard to tell when it happened.

⚡

Brownouts and weak signal

Cloud apps stop working when the power or internet drops, which is exactly when nobody is watching the numbers.

"I want to check my station anytime I want. Just tell my pump boy to take a photo of the current reading, and see what's really happening."

The v2 idea: random Spot Checks

The owner can ask for a check at any time of the day. Because the staff never know when the next request will come, the readings stay honest all day long, not just at closing time.

📲

Ask

Tap "Spot check Pump 2" from your phone, wherever you are.

📸

Snap and confirm

The attendant has 5 minutes to take a live photo of the meter, check the numbers the app read, and tap Confirm. Old gallery photos aren't accepted.

🔒

Only you know

The app compares the reading with sales so far and sends the owner a green "matches" or a red "short by X liters." Staff never see the result.

How it works, step by step

  1. You ask for a check. The attendant's phone gets a notice.
  2. The attendant photographs the pump meter with the app's camera only. The photo is stamped with the time and place.
  3. The phone reads the numbers itself. Reading a meter photo needs no internet, so it works in a weak-signal area.
  4. The attendant checks the numbers the app read against the meter, fixes any misread digit, and taps Confirm. Their screen only says "Saved, thank you."
  5. The app does the math with fixed rules, not AI guesses: liters on the meter now minus the opening reading, compared with liters sold so far.
  6. The result is sent only to you, the owner, right away if there's signal, or as soon as signal comes back. Nothing is lost while offline.
  7. Your phone's built-in AI explains it in Tagalog or English, for example: "Pump 2 is short 3.1 L between 2 PM and 5 PM. This is the third time this week on the afternoon shift."

The staff's only job is to confirm the photo was read correctly. They never see whether the station is short, so they can't change their answers to hide a gap. The comparison happens after they save and goes only to the owner.

What we learned from the hackathon

We studied the 9 winners and all 281 submitted projects. PumpLocal was the only gas station project, so the niche is wide open. Here's what the winners did better:

1. The AI did the main job

In v1 the AI only read photos and answered questions. In v2 it also spots patterns over many checks and writes the owner's daily summary.

2. It ran on a phone

Several winners ran their AI right on a phone in airplane mode. v2 does the same, so there's no laptop to carry or set up.

3. They talked about privacy

Sales, cash and staff records are business secrets. In v2 they stay on your phones, not on a cloud company's servers.

4. They showed it's big

The winners showed how many people had the problem. v2 is pitched to every remote station owner, not only JCB, with JCB as the real-world proof.

Can this go into the real JCB station app?

Yes. v2 is planned as a feature of the JCB station app (jcbgas-station.com), not as a separate app. That app already keeps your pumps, shifts and sales, so the Spot Check results land next to the numbers you already use.

  • A new "Spot Check" button for the owner, and a photo-and-confirm screen for staff.
  • Staff accounts can only upload and confirm. Results and shortages are visible to owner accounts only.
  • The meter reading and the matching math reuse what we built and tested in PumpLocal.
  • Results show up on your dashboard as a timeline: every check and every red flag, by pump and by shift.
  • It's added through the normal safe process: built on a separate copy first, tested, then switched on only after you approve.

Build steps, after your approval

  1. Step 1, Spot Check in the JCB app: owner request, live-camera photo, a staff confirm screen that shows no results, owner-only match results and history.
  2. Step 2, works offline: readings are saved on the phone during brownouts or no signal and sent automatically later.
  3. Step 3, AI on the owner's phone: pattern warnings and a daily Tagalog summary.
  4. Step 4, test at JCB Gas Station for 2 weeks with real attendants, and measure how accurate the readings are.
  5. Step 5, offer it to other owners, with JCB's real results as proof.
